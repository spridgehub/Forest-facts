Tracking data only exists from the 2025-26 season. High-speed running and sprint distance are the provider's two fastest speed bands. Metres per second = the whole team's distance divided by the actual playing time (kick-off to final whistle, half-time excluded) — divide by 11 for a rough per-player figure. More running isn't automatically better: teams that keep the ball often run less.
